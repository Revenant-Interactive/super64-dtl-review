A Founding seat is a digital membership only. It carries no ownership, equity, control, investment return, or prize rights.

Checkout is disabled. The proposed payment and cancellation language must match the owner-approved offer and reviewed refund-policy draft before sales open.

Why paid?

Because the vote has to mean something.

One paid member gets one ballot per window. Payment supports the data, security, and publishing infrastructure while reducing bots and coordinated ballot abuse. It never buys extra voting weight. This is a product model, not a current revenue or participation claim.
