The service

Super64 Club is a paid fan community: members vote weekly rankings, play a pick'em league for points and bragging rights only, and take part in club discussions. Super64 Club is an independent fan community and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any university, athletic conference, or the NCAA.

Memberships

Monthly and annual memberships renew automatically until canceled. Cancel anytime; access continues through the period already paid. The Founding 64 is a one-time purchase of a numbered lifetime digital membership that remains valid while the service operates. A Founding seat conveys no ownership, equity, control, investment return, profit participation, or prize rights of any kind.

Fair play

One account per person, one ballot per member per window. Automation, vote brigading, or account sharing may result in suspension. The pick'em league awards points and bragging rights only — no prizes of monetary value.

Merchandise orders

Product availability and price are confirmed during secure checkout. An item placed in a cart is not reserved. A checkout reservation may expire if payment is not completed. Shipping area, rate, tax, and delivery estimate are shown before payment. Super64 may cancel and refund an order that cannot be fulfilled.

Editorial information

College football articles, rankings, projections, and statistical analysis are for information and entertainment. Sources and retrieval times are displayed where applicable. Analysis is not betting, financial, recruiting, medical, or legal advice.

Conduct

Rivalry is welcome; harassment, hate, or threats are not. We may remove content or suspend accounts that break the club rules.

Liability

The service is provided "as is" without warranties. To the extent permitted by law, our liability is limited to the amount you paid in the twelve months before a claim.
