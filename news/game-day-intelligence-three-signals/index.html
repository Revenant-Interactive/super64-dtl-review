Three signals that tell you who is actually winning

Game intelligenceOriginal Super64 visual

Draft for owner review. This is not a live schedule, published result, membership promise, or event announcement.

A seven-point lead can hide a game that is slipping away. One team may be living on short fields and one broken play while the other is consistently staying ahead of the chains. Super64 Game Intelligence is designed to separate the score from the shape of the game.

The first signal is success rate: how often an offense gains enough yards to keep the drive on schedule. The second is explosiveness: whether a team is creating the chunk plays that flip field position and change win probability. The third is finishing drives: what happens after an offense creates a real scoring opportunity.

Those three signals matter because they describe repeatable control, sudden-strike danger, and execution when the field gets tight. They are more useful together than any single box-score total.
