How the weekly Super64 vote works

How it worksOriginal Super64 visual

Draft for owner review. This is not a live schedule, published result, membership promise, or event announcement.

The proposed ballot lets a member rank a Top 16 and nominate four teams to move into the Super64 field and four to move out. A documented Borda-style count converts submitted ballots into a weekly ranking.

The production rule is one member, one ballot per window. The final schedule and launch date remain pending owner approval; when a result is actually published, its snapshot will not be silently rewritten.
