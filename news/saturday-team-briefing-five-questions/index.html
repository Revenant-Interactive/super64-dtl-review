Concept draft

Five questions that explain your team before kickoff

Saturday briefingOriginal Super64 visual

Draft for owner review. This is not a live schedule, published result, membership promise, or event announcement.

A useful pregame briefing should leave a fan ready to watch the game, not buried under disconnected numbers. Super64 starts with five questions: Who has each team beaten? Which offense stays on schedule? Where do explosive plays come from? Which position matchup creates the biggest stress? What verified availability news changes the plan?

The answer is not one secret rating. Results establish what happened; efficiency shows how repeatable it was; opponent and game-state context explain what the box score misses. When live data is connected, every figure will carry its season, source, and retrieval time.

This framework also makes the postgame argument fairer. Fans can return to the same five questions, identify what changed, and update a Super16 ballot without pretending preseason reputation is a result.
