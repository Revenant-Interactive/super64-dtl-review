A ballot fans can defend

Four questions before you rank.

Super64 is independent and fan-voted. These lenses organize the argument; they do not copy an official poll or force one secret formula.

01

Results first

Start with wins, losses, and what happened on the field. Brand size and preseason reputation do not count as results.

02

Schedule context

A record means more when opponent quality is visible. Strength of schedule is context, not a substitute for winning games.

03

Direct comparison

Head-to-head and common-opponent results help separate otherwise comparable teams without making margin of victory the only story.

04

Current reality

Recent form and verified availability of key players or coaches can explain why the team taking the field now differs from its full-season average.

Methodology reference: the official College Football Playoff protocol explicitly considers schedule strength, head-to-head, common opponents, and key-player or coach availability when comparing teams. Super64 uses those as voter context, not as an official affiliation or copied committee result.

Proposed weekly cadence

From argument to permanent record.

The operating rhythm is production-ready but its final opening, closing, and publishing times still require owner approval.

  1. MON1

    Ballot opens

    Members rank sixteen programs from the current Super64 field.

  2. WEEK2

    Research & revise

    Ballots may be updated before the server-enforced closing time.

  3. SAT3

    Ballot closes

    One final ballot per active member enters the documented count.

  4. COUNT4

    Points are totaled

    #1 earns 16 points through #16 earning 1; deterministic tie-breaks settle ties.

  5. SUN5

    Snapshot publishes

    The aggregate result becomes a permanent dated week and is never silently rewritten.
