Our story

Built by fans. Designed to outlast a season.

Super64 is developing a custom 64-team field across four regions. It is an independent club format, not an official NCAA or conference alignment.

01

Who we are

Super64.club was founded by friends with a shared passion for college football. The goal is an honest, informed, welcoming community where supporters can debate the game, discover new perspectives, and represent their programs.

02

What we value

Respect for every fan. Accurate information. Useful analysis. Football for fun. Those principles guide the planned vote, the rankings model, and every future Super64 gathering.

03

Where it is going

Super64 can grow from a digital club into a lasting national community, with events, tools, original merchandise, and real-world gatherings that give the next generation a place to belong.

Club rules

Respect the game. Respect each other.

01

Respect every fan

Rivalry is part of college football. Personal attacks, harassment, hate, and threats are not.

02

Participate

When member voting opens, submit an informed ballot, share useful opinions, and help build a thoughtful community.

03

Have fun

Remember the founding rule: football for fun. Keep the competition passionate and the community welcoming.

Super64 Club is an independent fan community. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any university, athletic conference, or the NCAA.
